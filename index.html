Location

What You Can Expect

Adventure

From fieldcraft to camps, you will experience challenging and rewarding activities.

Skills

Build leadership, communication, and practical skills recognised beyond the squadron.

Service

Support community events, parades, and commemorations as part of the RAFAC.
