Privacy & Data Protection

Introduction

2324 Squadron RAF Air Cadets is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use personal data.

Data We Collect

When you use our website or contact the squadron, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone)
  • Information about your enquiry or reason for contacting us

How We Use Your Data

We use personal data to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Process applications to join the squadron
  • Communicate important squadron information

Data Protection

Your data is kept secure and confidential. We comply with UK data protection legislation, including the Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR.

Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy practices, please contact us.

Updates

This policy was last updated in March 2026. We may update this policy from time to time.
