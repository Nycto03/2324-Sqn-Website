Join Us

Begin your adventure with 2324 Squadron RAF Air Cadets. We welcome new cadets aged 12–17.

Get in Touch Squadron Details

Who Can Join?

Age

You must be between 13 and 17 years old (or 12 years old if in Year 8). Cadets can remain until their 20th birthday, after which they may take on staff roles.

Location

Live in or have easy access to Buckhurst Hill (we are a 15 minute walk from the station). You should be able to attend parade nights regularly.

Commitment

Willingness to participate in activities and follow squadron rules. No prior experiences needed!

The Joining Process

1. Enquire

Contact the squadron via the website or email to attend a recruitment induction. Speak to current cadets and officers about what to expect.

2. Induction

Attend an induction session to learn about the squadron, meet other recruits and cadets, and complete necessary paperwork with your parent/guardian.

3. Start Training

Begin recruitment training, complete your initial training to become a cadet and attend regular parade nights, and work towards your first badge qualifications.

Costs & What's Provided

Membership & Fees

Recruitment cost: Single £15 payment via Cash or Standing Order for entire 7 week recruitment period

Monthly subscription: £15 via Standing Order

Blue Uniform: Provided by the squadron; cadets are responsible for keeping it clean and serviceable.

Camo/Green Uniform: Not mandatory but some events require it; Single £100 payment via Cash only; cadets are responsible for keeping it clean and serviceable.

Activities: All parade-night activities are included. Camps and special events may have additional (subsidised) costs.

No hidden fees: We're committed to keeping costs accessible for all families.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience?

No! We welcome complete beginners. All cadets are trained from scratch regardless of background.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing and shoes for physical activities. Your uniform will be provided after the recruitment process.

Can I leave anytime?

Yes, there's no contract. However, we hope you'll commit to at least one term to settle in and enjoy the experience.
