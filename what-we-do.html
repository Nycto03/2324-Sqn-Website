What We Do

From tactical fieldcraft deployments to piloting aircraft, look at the experiences offered at 2324 Squadron.

Our Core Training Pillars

Flying & Aviation Opportunities

Take to the skies. Our cadets regularly fly in the Grob Tutor T1 light aircraft alongside experienced RAF pilots at Air Experience Flights (AEFs). You'll learn aerodynamics first-hand and even have the opportunity to take control of the aircraft during flight.

Fieldcraft & Military Exercises

Develop tactical field resilience. Under close supervision, cadets practice safe camouflage techniques, patrol patterns, team signal coordination, and field shelters construction. It builds leadership and real-world outdoor safety tracking mechanics.

Adventure Training & Athletics

Push your physical limits safely. We arrange regular trips for rock climbing, canoeing, windsurfing, mountain trekking, and multi-squadron competitive sports tournaments. Excellent context to finish DofE award requirements.
