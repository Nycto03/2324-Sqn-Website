What We Do

At 2324 Squadron, we offer diverse activities and opportunities to develop skills, build friendships, and have fun.

Our Activities

Ground Training

Learn fieldcraft, navigation, survival skills, and military knowledge during our Monday and Thursday parade nights, as well as regular events.

Annual Camps

Residential camps offer hands-on experience in adventure activities, teamwork challenges, and outdoor skills.

Flying Opportunities

Eligible cadets can experience the thrill of flying, competing for valuable air experience flights.

Adventure Activities

From rock climbing and canoeing to shooting and drill, we provide diverse outdoor and sporting activities.

Community Service

Support local events, parades, and commemorations. Be part of something bigger than yourself.

Leadership Development

Progress through ranks and potentially gain recognized qualifications in leadership and personal development.

Benefits of Joining

Skills for Life

Develop confidence, resilience, and practical skills valued by employers and universities.

Friendships

Meet cadets and officers from across your community in a supportive, inclusive environment.

Career Opportunities

Explore potential careers in the RAF and other fields through our extensive networks.

A Typical Parade Night

Monday & Thursday, 19:15–21:30

19:00–19:20: Arrival and First Parade

19:15–20:15: Main activity (drill, fieldcraft, adventure skills, etc.)

20:15–20:30: Break and refreshments

20:45–21:15: Secondary activity or training

21:15–21:30: Final Parade and dismissal, some cadets may be asked to stay behind to help tidy up the squadron, known as 'duty cadets'
