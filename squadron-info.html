Uniform & Standards

RAF Cadet Uniform

Cadets wear the official RAF Air Cadets uniform comprising No. 1 dress, No. 2 dress and No. 3 dress depending on the occasion.

Rank Structure

Progress through ranks from Cadet to Cadet Warrant Officer through demonstrated leadership, development and achievement.

Discipline & Values

We uphold RAF RISE values: Respect, Integrity, Service, Excellence. Cadets are expected to conduct themselves to high standards.
